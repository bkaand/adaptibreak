EYE ASPECT RATIO (EAR)
  Formula: EAR = (||p2-p6|| + ||p3-p5||) / (2 × ||p1-p4||)
  Implementation: Lines 52-66 in fatigue_detector.py
  Landmarks used: 6 points per eye (12 total)
  Threshold: 0.25
  Validation: 2 consecutive frames

MOUTH ASPECT RATIO (MAR)
  Formula: MAR = ||p2-p8|| / ||p1-p5||
  Implementation: Lines 68-87 in fatigue_detector.py
  Landmarks used: 8 mouth points
  Threshold: 0.6
  Validation: 15 consecutive frames

HEAD POSE ESTIMATION
  Method: 2D landmark projection to 3D angles
  Implementation: Lines 89-120 in fatigue_detector.py
  Tilt: arctan2(eye_y_diff, eye_x_diff)

  Thresholds: 15° tilt, 20° forward
